1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Z Share (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the Z Share Android application. Z Share is built for encrypted local peer-to-peer file sharing between phones and desktop companions over Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi Direct, and Local-Only Hotspot.

By using Z Share, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information & Files Processed Locally

Z Share does not operate backend file storage servers and does not upload, copy, or read your shared files on developer servers. All transfer payloads move directly between sender and receiver over your local network.

  • Shared Content: Photos, videos, audio files, documents, archives, text snippets, and APK packages you choose to send or accept are processed strictly on-device and streamed directly to the paired peer.
  • On-Device Transfer History & Trusted Peers: Z Share stores transfer logs (file name, byte size, MIME type, peer alias/ID, transfer duration, average speed, SHA-256 checksum, local destination URI, status, and timestamp) and trusted peer records (device ID, alias, TLS certificate fingerprint, last local IP address, and trust timestamp) in a local SQLite database (zshare_local.db) on your device.
  • App Preferences & Daily Quota Counter: Your custom device alias, theme settings, local server port, TLS/SHA-256/consent toggles, QR token expiration lifetime, daily free-tier byte counter (1 GB/day limit across send and receive), and subscription entitlement status are stored locally via Android DataStore Preferences.
  • Temporary Transfer Cache: In-progress chunked uploads and checksum verification files are stored temporarily in your device’s private app cache and cleaned up after completion or manually via Settings → Storage & Cache.

3. Permissions & Why We Request Them

Z Share requests only the Android permissions required for local device discovery, QR pairing, and reliable background transfers:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely when you open the QR Scanner to scan a peer’s pairing QR code. QR detection runs locally on-device via Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning and ZXing. Camera frames are never saved or transmitted to any server.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi & Local Networking (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES): Used to discover nearby Z Share peers on the same network via mDNS/NSD and UDP multicast, host the local HTTPS/TLS transfer server, and establish Wi-Fi Direct or Local-Only Hotspot connections. On Android 13+, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES is declared with neverForLocation.
  • Legacy Location Permission for Wi-Fi Discovery (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, Android 12 and below only): Required by older Android versions exclusively to scan Wi-Fi Direct / Local Hotspot peers. Z Share does not access, track, store, or share your physical GPS location.
  • Media & File Pickers (Zero Broad Storage Permissions): Z Share uses Android’s privacy-preserving Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework so you grant access only to the specific files or folders you select.
  • Installed Launcher Apps Query (MAIN / LAUNCHER intent query): Used locally only when you open the Share Apps (APK) screen to list installed apps you choose to share. Your app list is never uploaded.
  • Foreground Service, Notifications & Wake/Wi-Fi Locks (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE): Keeps large transfers reliable when the screen turns off or the app moves to the background, and displays dismissible transfer progress/completion notifications.

4. Third-Party Services (Ads & Subscriptions)

Although local file transfers work offline without any third-party network calls, Z Share uses the following Google services when an internet connection is available:

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Z Share integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display interstitial advertisements after completed transfers when online. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and coarse/network-derived region
  • App interactions, ad impressions, clicks, and crash/performance diagnostics

To learn how Google uses data from partner apps, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising anytime in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

B. Google Play Billing (Subscriptions)

Z Share offers an optional Z Share Unlimited Annual subscription (for example, ₹800/year in India or $20/year globally) to unlock unlimited daily data transfers beyond the 1 GB/day free tier. All purchases, billing, renewals, and cancellations are processed securely by Google Play. Z Share receives only purchase confirmation tokens and entitlement status from Google Play Billing and never receives or stores your credit card or payment credentials.

C. On-Device QR Processing (Google ML Kit)

Z Share uses Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning (bundled) for fast QR scanning. QR code decoding is performed locally on your device.

5. Security Measures

  • Local transfers support encrypted HTTPS/TLS with certificate fingerprint pinning.
  • QR pairing codes carry ephemeral, time-limited tokens signed with HMAC-SHA256 and single-use replay nonces.
  • Received files are verified with streaming SHA-256 checksums and sanitized filenames to block path traversal.
  • Incoming transfers from unknown devices require explicit on-screen approval unless you enable auto-accept for a trusted peer.

6. Data Retention & Your Controls

Because your transfer records and preferences reside on your own device, you stay in full control:

  • Delete Transfer Records: Delete individual items from History or tap Clear All at any time.
  • Revoke Trusted Peers: Remove any trusted device under Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Clear Temp Cache: Remove temporary cache files under Settings → Storage & Cache.
  • Remove All App Data: Clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling Z Share permanently removes all local app databases and preferences (note: files already saved to your public Downloads folder remain until you delete them).

If you believe a child has provided personal data in connection with third-party ad services or support communications, please contact us so we can assist promptly.
